# The survivorship-free US replay (2026-10-07)

**Question.** Every figure on the lab's register came from today's constituents. That
means each positive number was an upper bound. Do the screen's results survive when
each past date uses the companies that were actually in the index then? How much did
survivorship inflate them?

**Answer.**
- No registered test passes on the survivorship-free universe.
- On the same window, survivorship inflated the closest miss (H1-63) by about a third.
  Its t falls from 2.24 to 1.30.

Registration: `docs/workflow/backtest.md`, "Pre-registered point-in-time tests",
commit `e7fdb0b`. It was committed before either run.

Evidence: `docs/research/2026-10-07-evidence/pit-9y.json` and `sample-9y.json`, with
their logs.

**`DOW@2019-04-02`, diagnosed 2026-10-08.** It was not a rename: Dow Inc. has traded as
`DOW` on NYSE since 2019-04-01, and Dow Chemical held the symbol until 2017-08-31. Keyed
probes (`symbols=DOW`, `timeframe=1Day`, `adjustment=all`, `feed=sip`, `end=2026-10-07`),
one symbol per request:

- (A) `asof=2026-10-07`, `start=2016-06-29`: HTTP 200, 2583 bars, first 2016-06-29, last 2026-10-07
- (B) `asof=2026-10-07`, `start=2019-04-01`: HTTP 200, 1891 bars, first 2019-04-01, last 2026-10-07
- (C) no `asof`, `start=2016-06-29`: HTTP 200, 2583 bars, first 2016-06-29, last 2026-10-07
- (D) `asof=-`, `start=2016-06-29`: HTTP 200, 2583 bars, first 2016-06-29, last 2026-10-07
- (E) `asof=2019-04-02`, `start=2016-06-29`: HTTP 200, 2583 bars, first 2016-06-29, last 2026-10-07

(A) alone answers, so the single request does not reproduce the failure. The run's request
was not a single one, though. Replaying the run's real 100-symbol chunk (today's members,
`start=2016-06-29`, 25 pages) gives DOW **0 bars**, whatever the `end` format or `asof`
date. DOW plus 5 or 20 of those symbols still prices it, and DOW plus 50 (13 pages) does
not. The same 100-symbol chunk from `start=2019-04-02` returns 1890 Dow Inc. bars from
2019-04-02. So the empty answer needs both conditions: a large multi-page batch, and a
window that reaches back into the previous holder's years. It is reproducible, so it is
not transient.

The 2583-bar single-symbol series is also not Dow Inc. Its raw closes are Dow Chemical's
up to 2017-08-31 (66.65). Then come zero-volume bars, flat at that close, through
2019-04-01, and then Dow Inc. from 2019-04-02 (56.25). (B)'s first bar, 2019-04-01, is one
of those fills. That splice is why the mapping is never switched off and why a request
from 2016 is never the fix. Since the commit that adds this note, `loadPIT` asks for an
interval that its batch left empty once more, alone, from the interval's own start and
under the same `asof`, so DOW is priced from 2019-04-02 with Dow Inc. bars only. The
registered PIT tests above were not re-run and are not re-decided. The next PIT run
includes DOW@2019-04-02.

Both runs used the US indices only. The point-in-time membership comes from
`internal/universe/data/history`, priced through Alpaca's `asof` (see the README there).

## PIT-gap: how much survivorship inflated the US numbers

| Statistic | sample − point-in-time |
|---|---|
| β-adjusted IC10 | +0.0042 (from slightly positive to slightly negative) |
| H1-63 mean per hold | **+1.26 percentage points** (3.56% vs 2.30%); t 2.24 → 1.30 |
| E1 years positive | +1 (4 vs 3 of 10) |
| H1-15 mean per hold | +0.23 points |

The gap runs the way survivorship predicts on every row. The sample's missing
companies are disproportionately the ones that fell, and putting them back lowers every
long-tilted, momentum-ranked figure. The original 10-year, three-region H1-63 (t 2.31,
the register's closest miss) was the figure most exposed to this. Its US part, measured
cleanly, is no longer close.

## Decision (fixed in the registration)

All three fail. Therefore:
- The docs, CLAUDE.md and the TUI no-edge line now add that a survivorship-free US
  replay confirms it.
- Every US figure on the register should be read as an upper bound that survivorship
  inflated by about the gap above.

No out-of-sample registration follows from this run. OOS-H1-63 was registered before it
and stands unchanged: it uses a frozen universe, which is survivorship-free by
construction.

## Caveats, stated rather than buried

- **Weaker bar.** Only the US was run, so "every region" reduces to the US. That bar is
  weaker than C4's and H1's three-region bars, and every test failed it anyway.
- **Shorter window.** Nine years rather than ten, because Alpaca's history starts in 2016.
- **Sectors.** Departed companies have no sector, so 83% of S&P member-weeks are placed.
  None of the registered statistics uses sector.
- **No earnings signals.** `drift` and `earn_window` are not computed in this mode, because
  SEC resolves today's tickers only.
