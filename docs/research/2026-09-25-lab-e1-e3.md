# Lab experiments E1–E3: long history, sector-cap grid, long vs short

Plan: `docs/plans/2026-09-25-inputs-and-lab-before-edge.md`, §4 (Wave B), task 8. The tests and
their decision rules were registered in `docs/workflow/backtest.md` ("Pre-registered lab tests (Wave
B, registered 2026-09-25)") before any of them ran on real data. This note records the one run of
each and applies those rules as written.

**Outcome: no change on any of the three.** E1's beta-adjusted top-5 excess is positive in 7 of 11
calendar years, so the pre-registered trigger for withdrawing the edge language did not fire. None
of E2's three cap alternatives gets near |t| = 2.5, so `max_per_sector` stays at 2. E3's shorts are
negative beta-adjusted in the first half but positive in the second, so "long-only merit_veto" is
not pre-registered.

## How the numbers were produced

- Binary built from commit `b5c955e` (`go build -o <scratch>/cfr ./cmd/cfr`) and run with the repo
  as the working directory, so the shared data cache was `.data/`. Every run was started as
  `env -u ALPHAVANTAGE_API_KEY -u FRED_API_KEY -u DEEPSEEK_API_KEY -u CFR_API_KEY`.
  `cmd/cfr/backtest.go` builds only `marketdata.NewYahooClient` (keyless). No model was called and
  no credential was read.
- There were four invocations, all on 2026-09-26. The raw output and the stderr logs are in
  `docs/research/2026-09-25-evidence/backtest/`:

  | Invocation | Output | Saved report |
  |---|---|---|
  | `cfr backtest --years 10 --json` | `backtest-10y.json` | `.data/backtest/2026-09-26T15-03-02.json` |
  | `cfr backtest --years 10` | `backtest-10y.txt` | `.data/backtest/2026-09-26T15-14-13.json` |
  | `cfr backtest --json` (default 4 years) | `backtest-4y.json` | `.data/backtest/2026-09-26T15-14-29.json` |
  | `cfr backtest` | `backtest-4y.txt` | `.data/backtest/2026-09-26T15-15-35.json` |

  The first invocation fetched the 11-year daily series from keyless Yahoo, and the rest were
  served from cache. The two 10-year reports are identical once `generated_at` is removed (checked
  with `jq -S 'del(.generated_at)'` and `cmp`), so the text file and the JSON file describe the
  same computation. `run-log.txt` is the stderr of all four.
- **The 10-year sample is sound.** It has 521 weekly rebalances, from 2016-10-07 to 2026-09-25,
  with the second half starting on 2021-10-01. The panel has 136,478 rows, and 244 symbols returned
  with **0 unavailable** (`backtest-10y.json` lines 5–13, `run-log.txt` line 6). The requested start
  was 2016-09-25, and `shortfall` is absent, so the text report printed no `WARNING:` line. An
  earlier `--years 10` run at `5b2e16d` was **invalid**, because Yahoo's `range=max` served
  3-month bars. Its numbers are not used anywhere here.
- **The 4-year comparison sample** has 208 rebalances, from 2022-10-07 to 2026-09-25, with the
  second half starting on 2024-10-04. The panel has 55,612 rows and 0 symbols were unavailable
  (`backtest-4y.json` lines 5–13). This is one week more than the 2026-09-23 run's 207 weeks.
- **Survivorship.** Both universes are today's constituents only. The deeper a cross-section sits
  in the past, the more its missing names are past losers, so every early-year figure below is an
  upper bound, and a weaker one than a recent year's (`backtest-10y.txt` lines 4 and 154).

**Count.** The beta-adjusted top-5 excess is positive in **7 of 11** calendar years (2016, 2017,
2018, 2020, 2024, 2025, 2026) and negative in 4 (2019, 2021, 2022, 2023). If the two partial years
are dropped, it is still **5 of 9** full years. Plain top-5 excess is positive in 8 of 11.

**Decision: the trigger did not fire.** The count is a majority either way, so the docs and the TUI
keep their current wording. That wording already carries C4's finding that most of the screen's
apparent return is market beta (`docs/workflow/backtest.md`, "What C4 adds"), and nothing in
`CLAUDE.md`, `docs/workflow/*` or `internal/tui` currently claims the screen has an edge.

**What the pass does not establish.** The rule counts signs. It does not ask for significance, and
E1 was registered without the |t| bar. Read against the numbers around it, the result is weak:

- The beta-adjusted composite IC10 over the whole ten years is 0.006, with a Newey-West t of 0.66
  (`backtest-10y.txt` line 122). IC15 is also 0.006, with t 0.54.
- One of the seven positive years is 2017, at +0.035%, which is a rounding error on a 15-session
  hold. Without it the count is 6 of 11, still a majority but by one year.
- The negative years are consecutive, 2021–2023. Three straight losing years is how a regime
  dependence shows up, not independent noise.
- The survivorship bias is largest in exactly the early years that make up the majority.

This is a factual note, not a reinterpretation: the pre-registered rule is applied as written, and
it passes.

## E2: live-book replay with a sector-cap grid

**Pre-registered decision:** *"a candidate `max_per_sector` value is adopted over the live default
only if it clears that bar; otherwise the live default stands."* The bar is two-sided: |t| > 2.5,
with the same sign in both halves and every region, on the per-week paired difference in
beta-adjusted book excess (candidate minus cap 2), with Newey-West lags at `nwLags(15)` = 4.

Book arms from the 10-year run, `backtest-10y.txt` lines 170–176 (`book_grid.arms`). The replay

not the registered statistic, and it comes with a lower mean.

## E3: which side carries the result

**Pre-registered decision:** *"if shorts are ≤0 beta-adjusted in both halves, pre-register
'long-only merit_veto' as a config test for the live shipped arm."*

The barrier study's picks, 15-session directional excess, gross, in %. 10-year run:
`backtest-10y.txt` lines 164–168 (`sides`, line 5123 on). 4-year run: `backtest-4y.txt` lines 157–161
(`sides`, line 5057 on).

| Slice | Long | Short | **Long β-adj** | **Short β-adj** | n long | n short |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 10y all | 1.230 | −0.353 | 1.026 | −0.419 | 6139 | 4209 |
| 10y H1 (2016-10 → 2021-09) | 1.469 | −1.072 | 1.329 | **−1.046** | 3241 | 1959 |
| 10y H2 (2021-10 →) | 0.963 | 0.273 | 0.689 | **+0.127** | 2898 | 2250 |
| 4y all | 1.317 | 0.126 | 0.930 | 0.136 | 2361 | 1727 |
| 4y H1 (2022-10 → 2024-09) | 0.683 | −0.380 | 0.404 | **−0.246** | 1065 | 1015 |
| 4y H2 (2024-10 →) | 1.837 | 0.850 | 1.362 | **+0.681** | 1296 | 712 |

**Decision: the trigger did not fire.** Beta-adjusted shorts are negative in H1 and positive in H2,
in both the 10-year and the 4-year samples, so they are not ≤0 in both halves. "Long-only
merit_veto" is **not** pre-registered as a config test. For the record, the long side carries
most of the result: the long side is positive beta-adjusted in every slice of both runs, and the
10-year short side is negative overall at −0.419%. That observation changes nothing, because the
pre-registered rule is about the halves. It also matches the barrier study, where the 10-year hold
rule makes long +1.785% against short −1.225% (`backtest-10y.txt` line 159).

## Multiple-testing ledger

These are the Wave B statistics looked at on 2026-09-26. The five **decision** tests below are the
registered ones, run on the 10-year sample:

| Test | Decides | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| E1 | edge language in docs/TUI | 7/11 years positive → no change |
| E2-1 | `max_per_sector` = 1 | t −0.50 → fails |
| E2-3 | `max_per_sector` = 3 | t −0.61 → fails |
| E2-off | no sector cap | t 0.82 → fails |
| E3 | long-only config test | shorts H1 −1.046 / H2 +0.127 → not triggered |

That brings the lab's register to **8 decision tests** (C1, C3, C4 on 2026-09-23, plus these 5).
None has passed. Every lab run also recomputes C1, C3 and C4 automatically, which is why both
reports say `tests_run: 3`: that field counts only the C-series and does not include the E tests.
Those recomputations, on the 10-year and on the refreshed 4-year sample, are **6 further looks**
that decide nothing. All fail the bar. C1 is the closest, at t 1.44 on 10 years and 1.62 on 4 years
(`backtest-10y.txt` line 183, `backtest-4y.txt` line 176). The 4-year run's E2
and E3 figures are 4 more comparison-only looks. In total, 15 statistics were read on 2026-09-26,
and none of them changes a setting.

## Default window versus long window

Sources: `backtest-4y.txt` lines 8, 10, 122, 152 and 155; `backtest-10y.txt` lines 8, 10, 122, 159
and 162.

The composite's beta-adjusted IC stays indistinguishable from zero in both.
