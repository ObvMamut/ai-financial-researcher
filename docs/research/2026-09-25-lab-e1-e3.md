# Lab experiments E1–E3: long history, sector-cap grid, long vs short

Plan: `docs/plans/2026-09-25-inputs-and-lab-before-edge.md`, §4 (Wave B), task 8. The tests and
their decision rules were registered in `docs/workflow/backtest.md` ("Pre-registered lab tests (Wave
B, registered 2026-09-25)") before any of them ran on real data. This note records the one run of
each and applies those rules as written.

**Outcome: E1 fires; E2 and E3 change nothing.** Net of the lab's own 30bp cost, the screen's
beta-adjusted top-5 excess is positive in only 5 of 11 calendar years (3 of 9 full years), so the
docs stop describing the screen as having an edge and the TUI says so. Gross of cost it would have
been 7 of 11 and passed; the rule was pinned to net after the gross figures had been read (see E1).
None of E2's three cap alternatives gets near |t| = 2.5, so `max_per_sector` stays at 2. E3's
shorts are negative beta-adjusted in the first half but positive in the second, so "long-only
merit_veto" is not pre-registered. No selection setting or default changed.

- **Survivorship.** Both universes are today's constituents only. The deeper a cross-section sits
  in the past, the more its missing names are past losers, so every early-year figure below is an

## E1: long history

**Pre-registered decision:** *"if the composite's beta-adjusted top-5 excess is not positive in a
majority of years, the docs stop describing the screen as having an edge, and the TUI says so."*

**Rule as applied.** The registered sentence does not say gross or net, or which years count. After
the gross table below had been read (and reported, in `b326f3f`, as a pass), the final branch
review pointed out that the figure was gross while the lab charges every top-quintile and barrier
trade 30bp once. The controller ruled that E1 is read **net of that 30bp** — an edge that does not
survive the lab's own trading cost is not an edge, and every other top-trade figure in the lab is
quoted net — over **every calendar year in the replay**, with the full-year count reported beside
it. This is stated plainly because the pinning came after the result was seen and it reverses the
outcome: gross passes, net fails. `docs/workflow/backtest.md`'s E1 row records the same.

**Count.** Net, the beta-adjusted top-5 excess is positive in **5 of 11** calendar years (2016,
2020, 2024, 2025, 2026) and in **3 of 9** full years (2020, 2024, 2025). The report prints the
verdict itself (`backtest-10y.txt` line 184; `decisions`, JSON line 5430 on). Gross, the same column
is positive in 7 of 11 (5 of 9 full years).

**Decision: E1 fires.** Neither count is a majority. The consequence registered for this case is
applied: the docs stop describing the screen as having an edge, and the TUI says so. A grep of
`CLAUDE.md`, `docs/workflow/*.md`, `agents/*.md` and `internal/tui/*.go` for
`edge|alpha|outperform|beats?|predictive|predicts` (excluding EDGAR, hedge and ledger) found no
sentence claiming the screen has an edge: the hits are registered hypotheses (C4 and E1 rows), the
risk gate's `edge_sigma_daily` prior and the scoreboard's assumed-edge swap, claims about the model
stages, and one phrase in `backtest.md`'s E2 section ("overstates this screen's edge"), which now
reads "overstates what this screen earns". `CLAUDE.md` now states the E1 result, and the results
view of a legacy independent run carries one line: the backtest lab finds no edge in the pre-screen
that ranked these ideas, net of cost. No selection behaviour or default changed.

**How fragile the gross pass was.** The gross majority rested on years the cost erases: 2017
(+0.035%) and 2018 (+0.286%) are both below 30bp. Without 2017 alone the gross full-year count is
4 of 9, already not a majority. The surrounding numbers agree with the net reading:

- The beta-adjusted composite IC10 over the whole ten years is 0.006, with a Newey-West t of 0.66
  (`backtest-10y.txt` line 122). IC15 is also 0.006, with t 0.54.
- The negative years are consecutive, 2021–2023. Three straight losing years is how a regime
  dependence shows up, not independent noise.
- The survivorship bias is largest in the early years, which are the ones that helped the gross
  count.

The 4-year run's per-year table (`backtest-4y.txt` lines 141–148) is net-positive in 3 of 5 years
(2 of 3 full years). It is a comparison look, not E1's registered sample, and decides nothing.

**Decision: the trigger did not fire.** Beta-adjusted shorts are negative in H1 and positive in H2,
in both the 10-year and the 4-year samples, so they are not ≤0 in both halves. "Long-only
merit_veto" is **not** pre-registered as a config test. For the record, the long side carries
most of the result: the long side is positive beta-adjusted in every slice of both runs, and the
10-year short side is negative overall at −0.419%. That observation changes nothing, because the
pre-registered rule is about the halves. It also matches the barrier study, where the 10-year hold
rule makes long +1.785% against short −1.225% (`backtest-10y.txt` line 160).

## Multiple-testing ledger

These are the Wave B statistics looked at on 2026-09-26. The five **decision** tests below are the
registered ones, run on the 10-year sample:

| Test | Decides | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| E1 | edge language in docs/TUI | net 5/11 years positive (3/9 full) → **fires** (gross 7/11 would have passed; rule pinned to net after that reading) |
| E2-1 | `max_per_sector` = 1 | t −0.50 → fails |
| E2-3 | `max_per_sector` = 3 | t −0.61 → fails |
| E2-off | no sector cap | t 0.82 → fails |
| E3 | long-only config test | shorts H1 −1.046 / H2 +0.127 → not triggered |

That brings the lab's register to **8 decision tests** (C1, C3, C4 on 2026-09-23, plus these 5).
None has passed; E1 fired. Every lab run performs all eight, and the regenerated reports say so:
`tests_run: 8` (the `b5c955e` reports said 3, because the field then counted only the C-series).
The 10-year run's C1, C3 and C4 are **3 recomputations** that decide nothing. The 4-year run is
**8 comparison-only looks**: C1, C3, C4, E2-1, E2-3, E2-off, E3 and its per-year E1 table (which
the earlier count of 15 left out). In total, **16 statistics** were read on 2026-09-26. The C
recomputations all fail the bar; C1 is the closest, at t 1.44 on 10 years and 1.62 on 4 years
(`backtest-10y.txt` line 188, `backtest-4y.txt` line 181). E1's firing changes wording only; no
setting changed.
